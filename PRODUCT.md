# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local coffee enthusiasts, remote workers, and foodies looking for a premium coffee and Japanese-inspired dining experience. They seek quality, ambiance, and comfort.

## Product Purpose

To digitally represent the physical ambiance of Suge Coffee | Eatery. It must showcase the premium menu, highlight house specialties, provide location/contact details, and ultimately drive foot traffic and reservations.

## Brand Personality

Sophisticated, minimalist, warm, Japanese-inspired, premium, and calm.

## Anti-references

- Loud, cluttered generic restaurant templates.
- Overly playful or cheap "fast food" aesthetics.
- Cold, sterile corporate modernism.
- Endless grids of uniform cards for the menu.

## Design Principles

- **Show, don't tell:** Let the typography and color palette convey the premium Japanese-inspired vibe.
- **Architectural spacing:** Use intentional, generous whitespace to create rhythm, matching the physical space's calm atmosphere.
- **Commit to the palette:** Embrace the stark contrast of charcoal black, warm cream, and subtle gold accents seen in the physical menus.
- **Typographic hierarchy:** Rely on strong, deliberate typography (Japanese mincho serif for display, humanist sans for body and UI) rather than boxes and cards.
- **Imagery as voice:** Every surface that implies food, space, or warmth must carry a real photograph. A colored block where a hero image belongs is an incomplete design, not restraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast compliance, especially for menu items on dark/light backgrounds.
- Clear, readable typography across all screen sizes.
- Keyboard navigable and screen-reader friendly structure for the menu.
